Planning the Algarve and Thinking About Lagos? Connoisseurs Prefer These 2 Spots Just 15 Minutes Away

By the Casa Solaris hosts 18 November 2026 5 min read

The Western Algarve has long attracted travelers seeking sunshine, dramatic cliffs, and an unforgettable coastal atmosphere. When planning a getaway to this corner of Portugal, Lagos almost always takes center stage—a lively town boasting a stunning marina and a rich maritime history.

On Instagram, it looks picture-perfect: yachts bobbing in the harbor, sun-drenched cobbled streets, and the buzz of a vibrant resort. Yet true connoisseurs and seasoned travelers who know the Algarve inside out often take a different approach: they head just fifteen minutes further west and choose the charming villages of Praia da Luz or Burgau as their home base.

Lagos: A Fantastic Spot to Visit, But Where Should You Stay Near Lagos?

There is no denying Lagos’s appeal—it is an exceptional destination for a day trip. It’s well worth visiting to stroll along the picturesque marina, enjoy lunch at a waterfront restaurant, and soak up the local culture. However, the longer you stay right in the center, the more you notice the traits of a larger hub:

  • A Busier, Urban Feel: Compared to the region’s traditional villages, Lagos has a noticeably larger, more commercial and port-like character.
  • Crowded Town Beaches: While the surrounding cliffs are breathtaking, the main beaches closest to the town center can get heavily crowded during peak months, making true relaxation harder to find.

That is why savvy travelers treat Lagos as an ideal destination for an afternoon excursion or evening dining, while choosing the serene coastline right next door for their accommodation and genuine rest.

Praia da Luz & Burgau: The Insider’s Resort Alternative to Lagos in the Western Algarve

Just a short drive west of Lagos, the pace shifts into an oasis of calm and authentic Portuguese charm. Here—in Praia da Luz and Burgau—you’ll find idyllic beaches, unspoiled nature near the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park, and peaceful surroundings tailored to discerning guests. Right in the heart of these coastal gems awaits Casa Solaris, offering boutique holiday apartments that blend high-end comfort with a welcoming, home-away-from-home atmosphere.

  • Praia da Luz: Famous for its wide, golden sandy bay framed by the iconic black volcanic cliff, Rocha Negra. Luz offers an elegant palm-lined promenade, fantastic restaurants, and a relaxed, unhurried vibe—all while keeping you close to golf courses and every highlight of the Western Algarve.
  • Burgau: Often affectionately called Portugal’s miniature "Santorini." This traditional fishing village is tucked neatly between dramatic cliffs, featuring narrow, car-free cobbled lanes leading down to a sheltered, crystal-clear beach. Time moves slower here, and morning coffee overlooking the Atlantic feels worlds away from a busy city.

The Best of Both Worlds: The Best Alternative to Lagos in the Algarve

You don't have to miss out on everything Lagos has to offer—enjoy its energy, boutiques, and marina on a leisurely day trip. But when it comes to where you wake up and unwind, choose a setting designed for true relaxation. Skip the urban bustle and embrace the smart alternative: explore Lagos by day, and stay in Luz or Burgau.

Lagos, Luz or Burgau: Questions

Where should I stay in the Algarve: Lagos, Praia da Luz or Burgau?

+

Lagos suits travellers who want a lively town with a marina, shops and nightlife on the doorstep. Praia da Luz is a resort village with a wide sandy beach, a seafront promenade and plenty of restaurants. Burgau is a small, quiet fishing village with a sheltered beach. Many visitors stay in Praia da Luz or Burgau and visit Lagos on day trips, since it is only 10–20 minutes away by car.

Is Praia da Luz a good alternative to Lagos?

+

Yes. Praia da Luz is about 10 minutes west of Lagos by car and offers a long sandy beach under the Rocha Negra cliff, a palm-lined promenade, restaurants and bars, and two large supermarkets, with a calmer pace than Lagos.

How far are Praia da Luz and Burgau from Lagos?

+

Praia da Luz is about 10 minutes from Lagos by car, and Burgau about 20 minutes. Burgau and Praia da Luz are 5 minutes apart.
