Praia da Luz guide

One of the Western Algarve's best-loved beach resorts

Praia da Luz sits on a sheltered bay between the old Fortaleza da Luz and the black cliffs of Rocha Negra, about 10 minutes from Lagos. A former fishing village with Roman remains and a 16th-century church, it has grown into one of the most popular resort towns on this coast, with a Blue Flag beach, a mosaic promenade and calm, shallow water that suits families.

Beach, restaurants and bars

In Praia da Luz, in addition to the fantastic beach where you can safely swim and enjoy all water sports, float rentals and boat excursions, you can also enjoy the great entertainment provided by the numerous restaurants and bars that this small village has.
