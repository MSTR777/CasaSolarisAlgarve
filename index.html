Casa Solaris · Western Algarve

Casa Solaris has two apartments on the same stretch of coast: one above the fishing village of Burgau, and one in Praia da Luz, five minutes east by car.

Both are owned and looked after by us, and both can be booked direct. Burgau is the quieter of the two, on the edge of the Costa Vicentina natural park. Luz has the long beach, the promenade and more going on in the evening.

What every Casa Solaris stay includes

Swimming pools

Minutes to the beach

An 8-minute walk to the sand from both homes.

Super-fast Wi-Fi, TV and Netflix

500 Mb/s fibre, download and upload, for remote work. Cable TV and Netflix for evenings in.

Kitchen and dining area

A fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher and washing machine, and a proper table for long meals together.

Ready for babies and kids

Travel cot and highchair free of charge on request. In Luz, a children's pool and a playground.

Spotlessly clean

Top marks for cleanliness in guest reviews. Towels, bed linen and beach towels included.

Easy access, free parking

Burgau: step-free, private garage. Luz: lift, free street parking. We can help with airport transfers and car hire.

Air conditioning and heating

Cool in summer, warm in winter, with a fireplace in Burgau.
