Winter & long stays · October–April

Overwintering in Burgau, Western Algarve: a sunny base from October to April

Mild days, quiet villages and a house set up for staying a month or more.

  • October–April
  • 15–20% off long stays
  • 28+ nights welcome
  • Pool open all year

From October to April, the Western Algarve is at its most peaceful. The summer visitors have gone, the light is low and golden, and Burgau and Praia da Luz return to the pace of the people who live here all year. Casa Solaris is set up for that season: heating and a wood-burning fireplace, a desk with a monitor, a south-west-facing terrace and a pool that stays open all year.

It suits people leaving a northern winter behind for a few weeks or a few months: remote workers, walkers and golfers, and anyone who would rather spend January in the sun.

Why come between October and April

Mild weather and plenty of light

Daytime highs are typically around 16 °C in December and January, with five to six hours of sunshine a day. It is mild enough to spend most of the day outdoors, with the fire waiting in the evening.

Village life in Burgau and Praia da Luz

Out of season, Burgau is quiet and lived-in. A few beach bars close for the winter, but shops and restaurants in Luz stay open, and Lagos, about 15 minutes by car, has year-round restaurants, supermarkets and its marina.

Set up for a long stay

Working from the house

A dedicated desk with a monitor and laptop stand sits in a quiet corner, and free Wi-Fi runs throughout the apartment. Mornings at the desk, afternoons on the cliff path.

Warm inside, sun outside

Air conditioning and heating keep the rooms comfortable, and the wood-burning fireplace is there for cooler evenings. The south-west-facing terrace takes the afternoon and evening sun, and the shared pool is open all year, 07:00–22:00.

Everyday practicalities

  • Washing machine and dryer
  • Fully equipped kitchen, with Nespresso, moka pot and French press
  • Private underground garage space
  • Ground floor and step-free: two bedrooms and two bathrooms for up to five guests

Walking holidays in Burgau: the Fishermen’s Trail from the door

The Fishermen’s Trail of the Rota Vicentina runs through Burgau, minutes from the house, and the gentle cliff path to Praia da Luz starts in the village. Trekking poles are there to borrow, and an outdoor shower and storage for boots and kit are waiting when you come back.

Cycling holidays in the Western Algarve: a base in Burgau

The lanes inland from Burgau are quiet in winter, and the Ecovia do Litoral coast route passes through Lagos, about 15 minutes away. The house has storage for kit, local cycling maps and routes, and a private garage space.

Golf near Burgau: four courses within 30 minutes

Santo António and Espiche are about eight minutes by car from the apartment; Palmares and Boavista are within 15–30 minutes. Green fees change through the year, so check rates for your dates.
